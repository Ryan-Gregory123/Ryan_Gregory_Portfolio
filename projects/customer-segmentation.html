Customer Segmentation & Revenue Analysis

I used R to combine customer, product, and transaction data and explore what the information could tell us about business performance and customer behavior.

Project overview

I brought together several related datasets so I could look at the business from more than one angle. I examined revenue and profit patterns, then used recency, frequency, and monetary (RFM) measures to group customers and compare their behavior.

Analysis

  • Cleaned and prepared source tables, then joined transactions to customer and product information.
  • Calculated and visualized revenue by product category and monthly revenue from 2022 through 2024.
  • Compared total profit by device type.
  • Scaled recency, frequency, and monetary (RFM) measures and used an elbow-plot approach to assess cluster counts.
  • Applied k-means clustering with four clusters and visualized differences in RFM behavior.
  • Aggregated monthly revenue and fit a linear model using a time index and month factor to explore trend and seasonality.

Tools & techniques

Rdplyrtidyrlubridateggplot2Data joinsK-meansLinear modeling

Project focus

What I found most useful about this project was seeing how the same dataset could support several different questions. The work moved from cleaning and joining tables to visualizing performance and grouping customers, showing how each step builds on the one before it.
