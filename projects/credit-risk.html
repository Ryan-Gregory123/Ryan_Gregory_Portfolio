Credit Risk & Predictive Modeling

I used this case study to explore how classification models can help assess credit-risk outcomes.

Project overview

I worked through the modeling process from preparing and exploring the data to fitting classification models and checking their performance on held-out observations. Comparing logistic regression with random forest methods gave me a chance to think about both predictive performance and how model complexity affects results.

Methods explored

  • Exploratory data analysis and preparation of applicant/loan-related data.
  • Training and testing predictive classification models, including logistic regression and random forest methods.
  • Comparing model performance using test data and considering a baseline for context.
  • Reviewing model behavior and variable importance, including the risk of overfitting.

Tools & techniques

RLogistic regressionRandom forestClassificationTrain/test evaluation

Model evaluation

The main takeaway for me was that a model should not be judged only by how well it performs on the data used to build it. Testing on held-out observations and watching for overfitting are important parts of deciding whether a model is useful.
